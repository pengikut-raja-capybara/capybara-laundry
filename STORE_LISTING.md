```text
Aplikasi kasir (POS) & pembukuan kas laundry 100% offline, rapi & bebas iklan!
```

### Deskripsi Lengkap (Full Description — maks. 4000 karakter)
```text
Kelola usaha laundry Anda jadi lebih rapi, cepat, dan terarah dengan CaNdry — Capybara Laundry POS!

CaNdry adalah aplikasi kasir (Point of Sale) dan pencatatan arus kas khusus UMKM jasa laundry yang bekerja 100% offline di HP Anda. Dirancang dengan tampilan modern yang bersih dan mudah dipahami: Anda bisa memakainya secara super simpel (cukup ketik nama pelanggan & berat kg lalu simpan), maupun memanfaatkan fitur lengkap seperti manajemen paket harga, status cucian, piutang/DP, promo, buku kas, hingga laporan PDF.

✨ FITUR UTAMA CANDRY:
• Catat Cucian Super Cepat: Cukup ketik nama pelanggan (atau pilih dari saran otomatis) dan berat cucian (kg), total tagihan langsung terhitung otomatis.
• Status Cucian 4 Tahap: Pantau dan ubah status cucian dengan satu ketukan (Diterima → Diproses → Selesai → Diambil) lengkap dengan filter status dan pencarian nota.
• Pembayaran Fleksibel (Lunas, DP, Bayar Nanti): Catat pembayaran tunai, transfer bank, atau QRIS, serta pantau nota yang belum lunas agar tidak ada piutang terlewat.
• Bagikan Struk Foto & WhatsApp: Tampilkan nota lengkap sesuai identitas usaha laundry Anda, lalu bagikan sebagai foto struk (PNG) atau kirim langsung rincian nota ke WhatsApp pelanggan.
• Buku Kas Pemasukan & Pengeluaran: Setiap pembayaran cucian otomatis tercatat ke pemasukan kas. Anda juga bisa mencatat pemasukan & pengeluaran operasional (deterjen, listrik, gaji, sewa, dll.) lengkap dengan kategori akuntansi dan lampiran foto bukti.
• Laporan Harian, Bulanan & Tahunan: Pantau arus kas bersih, grafik perbandingan pemasukan vs pengeluaran, rincian per kategori, dan unduh laporan dalam format PDF.
• Manajemen Paket Harga, Pelanggan & Promo: Tambah dan edit paket kiloan/satuan/express, data pelanggan, serta kode promo diskon (% atau nominal Rp).
• Kunci Kata Sandi & Backup Lokal: Dilengkapi proteksi kata sandi dengan sesi 1 jam agar nyaman dipakai, serta fitur Export & Restore cadangan data (.json) ke penyimpanan lokal.
• 100% Offline & Tanpa Iklan: Seluruh data tersimpan aman di perangkat Anda tanpa perlu koneksi internet dan bebas gangguan iklan.

Kreator: Angga Alfiansah
Tim Pengembang: Pengikut Raja Capybara
Publisher: ANGSA Development Studio
Open Source Repository: github.com/pengikut-raja-capybara/capybara-laundry
```

## 3. Teks Listing (English `en-US`)

### Short Description (max. 80 characters)
```text
100% offline laundry POS & cashbook app! Track orders, receipts & reports.
```
*(74 characters)*

### Full Description (max. 4000 characters)
```text
Manage your laundry business cleanly and effortlessly with CaNdry — Capybara Laundry POS!

CaNdry is a lightweight, 100% offline Point of Sale (POS) and cashflow bookkeeping app crafted for laundry shops. Start recording orders in seconds by typing a customer's name and laundry weight, or unlock full capabilities including multi-tier service packages, wash status tracking, partial payments (DP), promo codes, accounting cashbook, and PDF financial reports.

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• Fast Laundry Order Entry: Type a customer name (with auto-suggestions) and weight (kg) — totals calculate automatically from your primary service rate.
• 4-Stage Wash Status Tracking: Update laundry progress with one tap (Received → Processing → Ready → Picked Up) with clean status filters and search.
• Flexible Payments: Track Paid, Partial (DP), and Pay-Later receivables across Cash, Bank Transfer, and QRIS.
• Share Receipt Image & WhatsApp: Customize your shop's header, address, phone, and footer, then share crisp PNG receipts or send order details directly via WhatsApp.
• Automatic Cashbook & Accounting Categories: Paid orders automatically log into your cashbook, alongside manual income/expense entries with photo attachments.
• Daily, Monthly & Yearly Reports: View net cashflow, dual bar charts, category breakdowns, and export full PDF reports.
• Local Backup & Password Lock: Keep your data safe with local password protection (1-hour session lock) and JSON backup/restore.
• 100% Offline & Ad-Free: All data stays securely on your device with zero ads and zero tracking.

Creator: Angga Alfiansah
Developer Team: Pengikut Raja Capybara
Publisher: ANGSA Development Studio
```
